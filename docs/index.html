🌍Kinder Geo

A geography game for kids. The game names a place, and you find it on a colourful map that has no names on it.

Play now ▶ See the code

How it started

Kinder Geo is a vibe-coded app I made for my kid.

It started with a globe. My kid would bring the globe to me and ask me to call out a country, so he could find it. Then he came up with the idea of points.

I turned that whole back-and-forth into this game, together with him.

Later, his friends and cousins asked for other countries too. So the game grew: now it also has the states, provinces and districts inside eight countries.

How to play

🧭

Choose where

The whole world, one continent or the US states. Or turn the menu to its second page and pick a country to play inside.

👆

Find it

The game names a place. Tap it on the map, then press Check. There are no names on the map to help!

⭐

Score points

Ten places a game. The map flies to the answer, so you learn where it was, right or wrong.

Your pickPoints
The right place100
A neighbour of it50
On the same continent (or in the same part of the country)25
Anything else0

Not in the mood for a quiz? In Explore mode you spin the globe, tap any country and read about it.

Made for kids

🙈 No ads, no accounts

No sign-up, no cookies, no tracking. The game loads nothing from other websites, and settings stay on your device.

📱 Any screen

Phones, tablets and computers. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

💚 Free and open

Free to play and open source under the MIT licence. The code is on GitHub.
